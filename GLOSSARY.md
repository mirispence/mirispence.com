# mirispence.com

Personal portfolio of an artist and writer: artwork organised into galleries, books with chapters, and a contact channel for enquiries.

## Language

### Art

**Artwork**:
A single published piece of visual art with its image, title and description.
_Avoid_: Piece, image, illustration (as a model name)

**Gallery**:
A named, ordered set of Artworks; an Artwork may belong to several Galleries or none.
_Avoid_: Collection, album

### Commissions

**Commission Request**:
A visitor's enquiry asking the artist to make custom work, followed from first contact through quoting to completion or rejection. It stays one record throughout.
_Avoid_: Commission (for the enquiry), order, booking

**Commission Rate**:
A type of commission the artist offers, with a title, a description and a dollar range. An active rate appears on the commissions page.
_Avoid_: Price, package, tier

**Commissions Open**:
Whether the artist is currently accepting Commission Requests. While closed, the public site shows no sign of commissions.
_Avoid_: Enabled, available, feature flag
